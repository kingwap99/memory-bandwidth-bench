# Privacy Policy — Memory Bandwidth Bench

Memory Bandwidth Bench does not collect, store, or transmit personal data.

- No account, sign-in, or user identifier.
- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
- No network requests during normal use. The app reads local system information
  (chip name, memory size, core counts) and runs its benchmarks entirely on your Mac.
- Measurements stay in memory. A CSV export is written only to the location you
  pick in the save dialog.
- Nothing is shared with third parties because nothing leaves your Mac.

Questions or concerns: open an issue at
<https://github.com/kingwap99/memory-bandwidth-bench/issues>.
